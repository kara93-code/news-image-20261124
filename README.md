A single front page for Tuesday 24 November 2026, drawn as one picture:
COP31 ending in deadlock in Antalya, a factory fire killing 24 in
Banten, Indonesia, and drone strikes around Kyiv lead the page, with a
28-point peace plan, 25 sailors dead near the Strait of Hormuz, and 25
more stories from that Tuesday — Gaza, Venezuela, Bolsonaro's return
to prison, the AFCON court ruling, India–Nepal, Tajikistan leaving
the ICC, Lisbon's cost-of-living march, Verstappen in Las Vegas,
Alcaraz in Turin, and the World Chess Championship opening in Geneva
— spread over seven regions, with the date printed in red across the
top so the day is never in doubt.

The image is drawn with Pillow by `draw.py` into `assets/entry.png`;
`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) validates the PNG and
copies it to `/output/entry.png`. The program runs with no network and
writes identical bytes on every run.
