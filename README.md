Round 2 of the blind rounds: a rebuilt front page for Tuesday
24 November 2026, with a different angle from round 1. A dark header
band carries the date in gold; one full-width hero leads with COP31
ending in deadlock in Antalya; four illustrated fronts follow — the
Banten factory fire, drones over Kyiv, 25 sailors dead near Hormuz,
and Trump's 28-point peace plan — and "The Rest of the Day" digests
25 more stories from that Tuesday, from Gaza and Venezuela to
Verstappen in Las Vegas and the World Chess Championship in Geneva.

The image is drawn with Pillow by `draw.py` into `assets/entry.png`;
`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) validates the PNG
chunk by chunk and copies it to `/output/entry.png`. The program runs
with no network and writes identical bytes on every run.
